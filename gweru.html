Gweru Interior Design

Interiors designed to feel like home.

A home that grows with you.

Designed for modern family living.

A 3D interior visualization created for a gated community housing project in Gweru, Zimbabwe.

The project explores the interior character of the homes across the key living spaces — from the lounge and kitchen to the bedrooms and bathrooms.

Every space tells a story.

A home, room by room.

The visualization was developed to create a consistent interior language across the different spaces of the residence.

Each room was designed to balance functionality with a contemporary aesthetic, using furniture, materials, lighting and carefully composed layouts to communicate how the finished home could feel.

The Living Space

Where everyday life unfolds.

The lounge establishes the main character of the interior — a comfortable space designed around everyday living, relaxation and connection.

Furniture placement, lighting and material choices work together to create a balanced and welcoming atmosphere.

The Kitchen

The heart of the home.

This project was created without a client brief.

The kitchen brings together functionality and contemporary design, with a layout focused on practical movement, storage and everyday use.

The visualization explores how the cabinetry, surfaces, appliances and lighting come together within the space.

The Private Spaces

Made for moments of quiet.

The bedrooms were visualized as more personal environments, using considered furniture arrangements, materials and lighting to create spaces that feel comfortable and calm.

The bathrooms complete the interior experience with a clean, functional approach to the home’s private areas.
