A clearer way

your money.

POSB Internet Banking already had the foundation of a functional digital banking experience. The opportunity was to create a stronger visual language — one that could bring clarity to a large amount of financial information without making the experience feel overwhelming.

Working from the existing UX, I focused on shaping the interface through visual hierarchy, typography, colour, spacing, component design and information grouping.

The dashboard was designed around the way people actually use internet banking: checking accounts, reviewing transactions, managing beneficiaries and accessing everyday services — often within the same session.

Instead of treating the dashboard as a collection of individual features, I designed it as a single, organised workspace.

Accounts, cards, recent transactions, beneficiaries, payments and services are separated into clear visual areas, allowing users to scan the page and immediately understand where they are and what they can do next.

The result is a banking experience that feels structured without feeling rigid, and information-rich without feeling complicated.

The goal was never to make internet banking look complicated or futuristic.

It was to make it feel clearer, more confident and more considered.

By giving information room to breathe and creating a visual system around the existing experience, the interface turns everyday banking into something that feels simpler to navigate — and easier to understand.
