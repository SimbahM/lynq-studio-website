National Blood Service Zimbabwe

A space built to serve.

A 3D exhibition stand designed for the National Blood Service Zimbabwe, bringing together healthcare communication, public engagement and functional exhibition spaces within one connected environment.

The concept combines four interconnected gazebos to create an open, approachable stand that guides visitors through the National Blood Service experience.

Built to Connect

Designed for engagement.

The exhibition environment combines practical functionality with strong visual communication.

Teardrop Banners

Strategically positioned banners create strong vertical branding and help the stand remain visible within a busy exhibition environment.

Reception

A dedicated reception desk creates a clear point of arrival, welcoming visitors and directing them through the exhibition experience.

Equipment Tables

Tables equipped with healthcare equipment create interactive demonstration areas where visitors can engage with the services and processes of the National Blood Service.

Care in Motion

Healthcare, brought into the exhibition.

The stand incorporates real-world healthcare elements to make the experience more tangible.

Hospital beds and medical equipment transform the exhibition from a conventional information stand into an environment that communicates the realities and importance of blood services.

A parked ambulance extends the experience beyond the gazebo structure, reinforcing the connection between blood services, emergency response and patient care.

Built to be seen.

Presence that speaks.

The visual identity of the stand is carried through its vertical elements, branded structures and carefully positioned displays.

From the teardrop banners rising above the stand to the ambulance positioned alongside it, every element contributes to visibility while maintaining a clear and welcoming visitor journey.

From concept to space.

3D visualization was used to translate the exhibition concept into a complete spatial experience — exploring the structure, branding, equipment placement, visitor flow and overall presentation before the stand reaches the exhibition floor.
