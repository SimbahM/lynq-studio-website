Waterfalls Kitchen

A kitchen made for everyday living.

Project introduction

Designed to feel as good as it functions.

This kitchen was envisioned as a clean, modern space where every element has a purpose. The darker lower cabinetry anchors the room, while the white upper cupboards keep the space feeling bright and open.

Glass-fronted pantries and selected glass cabinet doors introduce transparency and visual depth, allowing the storage to become part of the overall design rather than simply disappearing into it.

Materials & Details

A study in contrast.

Grey Base Cabinetry

The soft grey lower cupboards create a grounded, sophisticated foundation for the kitchen.

White Upper Cabinetry

Bright white overhead units provide contrast while helping the space maintain a light, spacious character.

Glass Storage

Glass pantry doors and selected glass-fronted cupboards break up the solid cabinetry and create moments to display kitchenware and accessories.

Integrated Lighting

Subtle LED light strips are incorporated beneath and within the cabinetry, adding warmth while providing practical task lighting.

Lighting

Light, built into the architecture.

Rather than treating lighting as an afterthought, the design integrates light directly into the cabinetry.

LED strips illuminate the work surfaces and glass storage, creating a softer atmosphere in the evening while highlighting the kitchen’s materials, textures and geometry.

3D Visualization

Every surface tells the story.

Through 3D visualization, the design explores how materials, lighting and proportions come together in the finished space.

From the reflection of the glass to the subtle illumination beneath the cupboards, each detail was considered to communicate how the kitchen could feel in reality.

Luxury 3D Kitchen with Coffee Bar

A sleek and welcoming kitchen where style, storage, and everyday comfort come together.

This 3D kitchen design combines clean contemporary finishes with a practical layout designed for daily living. The space feels open and refined, with carefully placed cabinetry and surfaces that create a polished, uncluttered look while still keeping everything within easy reach.

A dedicated coffee area adds a warm, personal touch, creating the perfect spot to start the day or welcome guests. The glass-front cabinets elevate the design by displaying selected pieces, while integrated lighting inside the cabinets creates a soft glow that highlights the contents and makes the kitchen feel more luxurious, especially in the evening.
