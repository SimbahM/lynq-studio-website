CABS Mobile Banking

Banking, redesigned

A refined mobile banking experience for CABS, designed to make everyday banking feel simpler, clearer and more effortless.

One screen.
Everything within reach.

The homepage was designed around a clear hierarchy: the user's most important financial information appears first, followed by frequently used services and supporting utilities.

The visual system uses layered cards, rounded containers, strong typography and familiar iconography to create clear groupings without making the interface feel overwhelming.

Design language

Iconography

Simple, recognisable icons help users identify services quickly and reduce the amount of text needed to navigate the interface.

Colour

CABS blue establishes trust and brand recognition, while carefully selected accent colours help distinguish actions and services.

Design language

Hierarchy

Typography, spacing, scale and contrast work together to establish a clear order of importance on every screen.

Cards

Content is grouped into lightweight cards, creating clear visual boundaries while keeping the interface flexible and scalable.

Navigation

The bottom navigation keeps the core destinations consistently accessible, allowing users to move through the app without losing context.

Typography

Made to be read.

Typography creates the hierarchy of the experience.

From account balances and transaction amounts to service names and supporting information, type scale and weight are used to help users understand what matters first.

Inputs

Designed for effortless interaction.

Forms shouldn't feel like work.

Input fields, selectors and controls were designed with clear states, comfortable spacing and familiar interaction patterns so users can enter information with confidence.

Navigation

Navigation was designed to remain predictable throughout the experience.

Persistent navigation, clear labels and recognisable icons help users understand their current location and move between essential banking services without friction.

Layout

The interface follows a consistent visual rhythm.

Spacing, grids, cards, alignment and component behavior create a system that allows individual screens to feel different while still belonging to the same product.
