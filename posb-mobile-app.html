Banking, redesigned

A new visual direction for POSB Mobile Banking — bringing clarity, confidence and a more contemporary digital experience to everyday banking.
Design

A new look
for a familiar banking
experience.

POSB already had a functional mobile banking experience. My role was to give it a stronger visual identity — modern, approachable and distinctly digital.

Working from the existing UX, I focused on visual hierarchy, typography, colour, components and interaction patterns to make the experience clearer and more engaging.

Large cards, generous spacing, strong typography and rounded surfaces create a softer banking experience, while account information and key actions remain easy to find.

Every element was designed with purpose — from presenting balances to grouping and navigating services.

Take a closer look.

Built to feel familiar.
Designed to feel simple.

Good banking UI shouldn't make people think about the interface.

It should make the information feel obvious.

The visual language was built around a simple idea: reduce visual noise while giving important information more room to breathe.

Clear hierarchy

Balances, accounts and important actions are given strong visual priority. Large typography creates an immediate understanding of what matters most, while secondary information stays present without competing for attention.

A softer banking experience

Rounded cards, spacious layouts and subtle gradients introduce warmth into an environment that can often feel rigid and transactional.

The result is an interface that feels less like a financial dashboard and more like a product people enjoy using.

Colour with purpose

Color isn't used simply to decorate the interface.

The POSB brand becomes part of the experience through carefully controlled accents, service icons and visual states. Different colors help users recognize actions and categories while maintaining a consistent visual language.
